Institute of Aerospace Thermodynamics (ITLR), University of Stuttgart

Research

My PhD covers the ITLR part of a DFG project with Markus Klein's group (UniBw München) on DNS and LES of primary and secondary breakup of shear-thinning and thixotropic jets.
